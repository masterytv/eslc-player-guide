1What the app is

The Player Guide is the team handbook, on everyone’s phone. It replaces the Google Doc: everything a player needs for a tournament is in one place, and it’s always up to date.

  • Players use it to check the day’s plan, the schedule, who to call, where to go, what to pack, and the coaches’ game plan.
  • Staff can change anything in it from their phone. Players see the change the next time they open a page. Nobody has to download anything.
  • It works with no signal. Each phone keeps a copy, so the guide still opens on a plane or abroad without data.
  • One guide per tournament. When a tournament ends, staff start the next one and copy over what stays the same, like staff, the roster and the packing list.

2Getting in

Open eslc-player-guide.vercel.app in your phone’s web browser. You’ll see one box to type into. What you type decides what you can do:

Team passcode

For players. They can read everything, but can’t change anything. It’s shared in the team group chat.

Staff password

For coaches and staff. Lets you edit the guide. Tom sends it to you privately. Please never post it in the group chat.

Put it on your home screen

Then it opens like an app:

  • iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android (Chrome): tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to home screen.

You only log in once

The phone remembers you. Players are only asked again if staff change the team passcode.

3What players see

There are five tabs along the bottom of the screen. All times are local time where the tournament is.

  • TodayA countdown, the next game, and the day’s plan (buses, meals, meetings). An alert banner shows at the top when staff post one. Tap the days along the top to see another day. Quick links below the plan include Offence and Defence.
  • ScheduleEvery game, practice, ceremony and meeting. Players can add one game, or Add every game to my calendar.
  • TeamStaff, with Call and WhatsApp buttons once a number is added, plus the roster and the rooming list.
  • VenueThe airport bus and other ways to get there, the villas, the fields, maps and meals.
  • MorePacking list, flight details, the Game plan (offence and defence), tournament rules, conduct, the anthem and useful links.

The game plan

Each coach’s philosophy, in their own words, laid out as short cards. Players can open it from Quick links on Today or from More → Game plan, and it works offline. That means they can read it on the flight.

Packing list

Players tick items off as they pack. Ticks are saved on their own phone only, so nobody else sees them.

“Saved copy” and “Offline”

Sometimes a note at the top right says Saved copy or Offline. It means the phone is showing its saved copy. It updates by itself as soon as there’s signal, and players don’t need to do anything.

4Making changes

Log in with the staff password. An orange STAFF badge shows at the top, and orange Edit buttons appear on anything you can change.

Two ways in

  1. Tap Edit next to the thing you want to change. It opens straight to that item.
  2. Or go to More → Edit the guide for the full list, grouped as Today & schedule, Team, Venue & stay, and More.

In the editor

  • Tap an item to open it, change the boxes, and tap Save at the bottom. When it says Saved, players will see the change the next time they open a page.
  • To add something, use the Add a … button under the list. To remove something, open it and tap Delete; it asks you to confirm. Use the arrows to change the order.
  • A box outlined in red needs fixing, and the message under it says what to do.
  • If two staff edit the same thing at once, the second person gets a warning instead of wiping out the first person’s work.
  • No signal? Your changes stay on screen. Tap Save again once you have signal.

Pages (rules, conduct, activities) and the game plan use a simple text format. A reminder is shown under the text box:

5Starting a new tournament

Each tournament gets its own guide. You get the new one ready while players still see the current one, then switch everyone over in one tap.

  1. Fill in the detailsGo to More → Tournaments → Start the next tournament. Add the name, where it is, the first and last day, and the time zone: every time in the guide is shown in local time there. Choose the team passcode. If you keep the current one, players stay logged in; a new one signs everyone out.
  2. Choose what to copyPick a tournament to start from. Ticked parts are copied over for you to edit: staff, roster, packing list, pages, game plan, anthem and links. Everything else starts empty: schedule, daily notes, travel, villas, venue, maps, meals and rooming. Tap Start the tournament.
  3. Get it readyThe new tournament is a draft that only staff can see. An orange bar at the top says which one you’re working on and which one players see. Edit everything as usual; players still see the current guide. Tap Switch on the bar to move between tournaments.
  4. Make it liveWhen it’s ready, open More → Tournaments and tap Make live on the new one. Players see it the next time they open the guide. If the passcode changed, post the new one in the group chat first.
Old tournaments stay

Past tournaments stay in the Tournaments list, so you can look back at them or copy from them later. Players only ever see the live one.

6Good to know

Do players need to update or refresh anything?

No. The guide updates itself whenever they open it with signal.

Who can see what?

Anyone with the team passcode can read the whole guide, including staff phone numbers. Don’t put anything private in it, like passport numbers or medical details. Only people with the staff password can make changes.

Can I see which players have read something?

Not yet. Everyone shares one team passcode, so the app can’t tell players apart. To check, ask in the group chat.

I made a mistake. Can I undo it?

Before you save, just leave the page. It asks first, then drops your changes. After you save, change it back and save again.

Someone forgot the passcode

Staff can always see it under More → Team passcode, with a button to copy it.

Can the staff password be changed?

Yes, but only Tom can change it. Ask him if you think it has been shared too widely.

A player says the guide looks wrong or old

Ask them to open it with signal and wait a few seconds. If the top right still says Offline, their phone has no connection. If something still looks wrong, contact Tom.
